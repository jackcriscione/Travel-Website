Albany

Albany is the captial city of the state of New York. It is also one of the oldest cities in the United States, founded in 1686.

Recommended Places to Visit

  • NYS Capitol Building
  • Empire State Plaza
  • New York State Museum
  • Washington Park
  • U.S.S. Slater
  • MVP Arena
  • Crossgates Mall

Travel Tips

  • The train station across the river in Rensselaer is a great way to travel to Boston, NYC, and other major cities in NYS.
  • The CDTA bus system is a great way to get around, but renting a car will provide the most freedom to travel around.

New York City

New York City is one of the most famous cities in the entire world and is home to over 8 million people!

Recommended Places to Visit

  • Times Square
  • Central Park
  • The Statue of Liberty
  • The Brooklyn Bridge
  • The American Museum of Natural History
  • The Metropolitan Museum of Art
  • The 9/11 Memorial and Museum

Travel Tips

  • You can take the Staten Island Ferry to get an amazing view of NYC for completely free, including a view of the Statue of Liberty!
  • Taking the PATH train to Jersey City will also get you an amazing view of the city!

Niagara Falls

Niagara Falls is a city in the state of New York and also the name of a famous state park, featuring a group of 3 huge waterfalls.

Recommended Places to Visit

  • Niagara Falls State Park
  • The Maid of the Mist
  • Journey Behind the Falls
  • The Cave of the Winds

Travel Tips

  • Go to the Canadian side of the falls to get a better view.
  • Make sure to do a boat ride that gets close to the falls.
