Athens

Athens is the capital of Greece and one of the world's oldest cities.

Recommended Places to Visit

  • Acropolis
  • Parthenon
  • Acropolis Museum
  • Temple of Hephaestus

Travel Tips

  • Make sure to bring an umbrella and rainjacket, as it can rain a lot depending on when you visit. It rained a lot when I visited in April.

Santorini

Santorini is well-known for its stunning views, beautiful sunsets, whitewashed building, and blue-domed churches.

Recommended Places to Visit

  • Oia
  • Fira
  • Red Beach

Travel Tips

  • Use the cable lift to get from the old port to Fira.

Mykonos

Mykonos is known for its scenic views, historic windmills, and welcoming atmosphere. It is a popular island destination in the Aegean Sea.

Recommended Places to Visit

  • Mykonos Windmills
  • Little Venice
  • Mykonos Town
  • Paradise Beach

Travel Tips

  • Explore Mykonos on foot and visit in the off-season to avoid large crowds.
