Barcelona

Barcelona is one of the most popular cities to visit in Spain. It is well-known for its unique architecture, beautiful beaches, and rich culture.

Recommended Places to Visit

  • Magic Fountain of Montjuïc
  • Arc de Triomf
  • Basilica of the Sagrada Familia
  • Park Güell

Travel Tips

  • Make sure you purchase tickets for the Sagrada Familia days, or even weeks in advance, as they can quickly sell out! Tickets can be purchased online.

Seville

Seville is an extremely underrated city and was my favorite city I visited in all of Spain. The Royal Alcázar is featured in Game of Thrones, which I didn't realize until 5 years later when I started watching the show.

Recommended Places to Visit

  • Plaza de España
  • Royal Alcázar of Seville
  • Seville Cathedral

Travel Tips

  • Seville is one of the hottest cities in Europe, so make sure to bring a hat and a water bottle.
  • Find a restaurant right near the river and eat dinner there.

Granada

Granada is a historic city in southern Spain. It is home to the famous Alhambra, which is a stunning palace overlooking the city. Granada is also one of the best places in Spain to see a flamenco performance.

Recommended Places to Visit

  • The Alhambra
  • Granada Cathedral
  • Cueva de la Rocío
  • Nasrid Palaces

Travel Tips

  • The streets here can be extremely narrow and steep, so using taxi services is your best option.

Toledo

Toledo is a historic city in central Spain known for its medieval streets and well-preserved architecture. All 3 monotheistic religions (Christianity, Islam, and Judaism) have historical influences in this city.

Recommended Places to Visit

  • Toledo Cathedral
  • The Old Town of Toledo
  • Alcázar of Toledo

Travel Tips

  • Wear comfortable shoes, as you will do a lot of walking.

Valencia

Valencia is a vibrant and youthful city in Spain, well known for its modern architecture and paella.

Recommended Places to Visit

  • Oceanogràfic València (Aquarium)
  • Valencia Cathedral
  • City of Arts and Sciences

Travel Tips

  • Skip the aquarium if you are sensitive to smells, as it smelled bad almost everywhere inside and outside the aquarium when I visited. Seeing the dolphins, fish, and jellyfish made up for it though.
