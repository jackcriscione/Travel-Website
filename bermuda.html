Bermuda

Bermuda is an extremely popular island to visit, best known for its pink-sand beaches.

Recommended Places to Visit

  • Royal Naval Dockyard
  • The National Museum of Bermuda
  • Horseshoe Bay Beach
  • Tobacco Bay Beach
  • Hamilton, Bermuda
  • Saint George's Island

Travel Tips

  • The best way to visit Bermuda is via a cruise ship that stays overnight for multiple days.
  • Horseshoe Bay Beach is a must-see location!
